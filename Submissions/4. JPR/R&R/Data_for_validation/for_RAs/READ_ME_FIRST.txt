RAI ARTICLE VALIDATION — 17 NOVEMBER 2026

This folder is the packet for Diego's research assistants. It contains 1,600 articles in randomized order.

1. Extract the entire folder before opening its files.
2. Save a separate copy of RA_coding.xlsx for each coder. The Instructions sheet contains the coding guide.
3. Open article_reader.html in a web browser to read the available article text. It works offline. Enter the audit ID from the workbook, such as RAI0001, to open the matching article.
4. Record responses in the workbook's Coding sheet. Keep audit IDs unchanged. Articles and articles.csv preserve the supplied source text and metadata.
5. Code independently before discussing disagreements. Leave unreviewed rows blank; use the explicit uncertainty choices for reviewed but ambiguous articles.

Assess three different things: whether the story substantively concerns the outlet's country; whether it reports a relevant Russian or Chinese activity; and the actor's role and target/counterpart. A place of publication or a passing mention does not establish any of these. Sanctions against Russia do not make Russia their initiator. Both Russia and China can be discussed in the same article and must be judged separately.

Language markers reflect stored database metadata. They do not certify language detection or translation quality. The English body may be a stored excerpt. Original-language text is supplied where available. Report unreadable or incomplete evidence explicitly.

CSV text beginning with spreadsheet formula characters receives a leading apostrophe for safe import. The HTML reader retains the original supplied text. The analyst JSON preserves the raw extracted fields.

Automated strata and predictions are intentionally provided only to the project investigators. They are not answer keys for the RAs.
